META trace 2.2.0-rc.1 (release candidate)
Real local EXIF/GPS/XMP extraction with a dark-red analysis presentation and an interactive 3D globe: a located photo plays
one camera path (full revolution, descent, hand-off) to a square frame exactly five miles by five miles centred on the
